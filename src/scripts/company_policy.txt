SECTION HR-001

Category: Leave Policy
Department: Human Resources
Allowed Roles: Employee, Manager, HR
Sensitivity: Internal

Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year.

Rules:

Leave must be applied via the HR portal.

Approval from direct manager is mandatory.

Maximum carry forward allowed: 10 days.

HR has override authority in exceptional cases.

SECTION HR-002

Category: Sick Leave
Department: Human Resources
Allowed Roles: Employee, Manager, HR
Sensitivity: Internal

Employees are entitled to 12 sick leave days annually.

Medical certificate required if leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.

HR may request documentation for pattern-based misuse.

SECTION SEC-002

Category: Data Breach Protocol
Department: Security
Allowed Roles: IT, Security, Admin
Sensitivity: Highly Confidential

In case of data breach:

Incident must be reported within 24 hours.

IT must isolate affected systems.

Security team conducts forensic analysis.

Admin notifies regulatory bodies if required.

Employees must not communicate breach details externally.

SECTION ADMIN-001

Category: Termination Procedure
Department: Administration
Allowed Roles: Admin, HR
Sensitivity: Confidential

Upon resignation:

Notice period: 60 days.

IT must revoke system access on last working day.

Finance processes final settlement within 30 days.

Assets must be returned before clearance.
